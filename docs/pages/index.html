Shule Timetable

Synthetic sample data

A constraint solver builds a clash-free school timetable from plain CSV and YAML, then an independent validator re-checks every hard rule on the result. These pages are the generated output for two invented schools.

Secondary school16 classes, 29 teachers, labs. Solved with a 120 s limit; the page states the measured gap. Primary school6 classes, 8 teachers, class teachers plus two specialists.
