Shule Timetable

Synthetic sample data. Both schools, and everyone in them, are invented.

A constraint solver builds a school timetable from plain CSV and YAML files and prints a page for every class, teacher and room.

A separate validator then re-checks every hard rule on the finished timetable. The pages below are that generated output.

Things to try

  1. Use "Where am I teaching?" on a school's home page to find a teacher or class at a given day and period.
  2. Open a teacher page on your phone: it becomes a plain list per day.
  3. Print a class page or the master grid on A4 landscape. Every subject has a pattern as well as a colour, so a black and white copy still reads.

The checks prove each timetable obeys the rules as written in its input files, not that those rules describe a real school.
